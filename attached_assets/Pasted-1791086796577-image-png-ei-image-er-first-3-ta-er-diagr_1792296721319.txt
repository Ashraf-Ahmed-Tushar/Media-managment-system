Spotting the missing entity behind a dangling foreign key.
Tomar list-er attribute (column) ekdom thik ache. Amar ER relation-gulo banate jei column dorkar chhilo, shob tomar list-e ache, kono column missing na. Ekta entity missing ar kichu FK mark missing.

1. Missing entity: CUSTOM_ROLES
USER_CUSTOM_ROLES.custom_role_id FK → CUSTOM_ROLES.id bole, kintu CUSTOM_ROLES tomar 14 entity-r list-e nei. Ota na thakle FK ta kothao point korche na. Tai entity hobe 15 ta:

Entity 15 — CUSTOM_ROLES
id (PK)
name
description
created_at
updated_at
Ei column-gulo age-er image theke niyechi, tomar doc-e nei. Tomar project-e ja ache seta mile dekho. Report-er “fourteen entities” o “fifteen” korte hobe.

Tomar list-e jegulo already ache (BOOKS.created_by, USER_CUSTOM_ROLES duto, PLAYLISTS.category_id, BOOK_PLAYLISTS.category_id) sheguloi thik.

3. Tomar decision lagbe
CATEGORIES.parent_id: eta CATEGORIES.id-ke point kore (self-reference), ar na hole FK mark korte hobe: parent_id (FK → CATEGORIES.id).
VIDEOS.subcategory_id: jodi sub-category-o CATEGORIES table-er row hoy, tahole FK → CATEGORIES.id hobe. Na hole je table-e ache seta bolo.
FAVORITES.content_id: eta content_type onujayi BOOKS ba VIDEOS-e jay, tai FK dewa jabe na. Report-e “polymorphic reference, no FK” likhe dile bhalo.
Optional: PLAYLIST_ITEMS-e UNIQUE(playlist_id, video_id) ar BOOK_PLAYLIST_ITEMS-e UNIQUE(playlist_id, book_id) dile ekoi item duibar playlist-e dhukte parbe na. Eta tomar list-e nei.
4. Amar diagram-e ja update lagbe
Tomar notun list-e USERS.email (UNIQUE) ar CATEGORIES.slug (UNIQUE) ache. Eta amar ER ar Schema diagram-e nei. Tumi bolle ei duto UNIQUE, CUSTOM_ROLES-er jaygay tomar final column, ar upor-er decision-gulo diye 3 ta diagram abar update kore dibo.
